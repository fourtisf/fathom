TITLE: Introducing Noxsea: Private AI That Forgets You

Every day, millions of people ask AI chatbots things they would never post publicly. Which wallet to move their funds to. Whether a token is a rug. How to handle a tax problem. A health worry at 2am.
Most of those conversations are stored. Many are used to train future models. Some may be read by people.
We built Noxsea because we think your questions should stay yours.

## What is Noxsea?

Noxsea is a private AI assistant built for the onchain world. You chat with leading open-weight models, sign in with your wallet, and stay in control of what is saved, and for how long.

- No email, no password, no KYC. One wallet signature is your account.
- Zero prompt logs. What you ask is never written to our logs or database in readable form.
- Open-weight models. DeepSeek V3.1, Qwen3 235B, gpt-oss 120B and Llama 3.3 70B.

## Private by design, not by promise

Privacy is easy to claim and hard to build. Here is what actually happens to a message on Noxsea:

- Encrypted in transit. Your browser sends it over HTTPS.
- Relayed, never logged. Our server checks your credits and forwards it. The message is never written to logs or the database.
- Answered by an open model through inference providers that are required not to store prompts or train on them.
- Saved only if you choose. With history on, chats are encrypted in your browser with a key derived from your wallet signature. We store only ciphertext we can't read.

You can also set any chat to self-destruct after 1 hour or 24 hours, or forget it instantly with one click. We publish where your data goes, step by step, at noxsea.xyz/trust.

## Built for crypto

General chatbots don't know what happened onchain this morning. Noxsea comes with tools for people who live onchain:

- Token Safety Check. Paste a contract address on Robinhood Chain and get the red flags in seconds: whether new tokens can be minted, whether wallets can be blacklisted, whether taxes can be changed, who owns the contract, whether its code can be upgraded, and how concentrated the top holders are. The AI explains what each flag means. It never calls a token "safe", and it isn't financial advice.
- Live prices. Ask about BTC, ETH, SOL or any $TICKER and get live market data instead of outdated training data.
- Read PDFs privately. Attach a whitepaper or an audit. It's read inside your browser and never uploaded; only the text goes into your question.
- Compare mode. Send one question to two models and read both answers side by side.
- Expert modes. Switch to Contract auditor, Memecoin researcher, Web3 developer, Crypto writer or Explain simply in one click.
- Encrypted share links. Share a chat with a link that carries its own key. We only ever see ciphertext, and links expire after 1 to 30 days.

Outside lookups for on-chain data and prices leave from our servers, so your IP address is never exposed. Only the address or the coin name is sent, never your message.

## Pay only for what you use

No subscriptions. Each message costs credits based on the tokens it actually uses, and failed requests are never charged. New wallets with on-chain activity get 25 free credits to try every model. Top-ups in USDG on Robinhood Chain are coming soon.
